ReleaseBound

Independent release verification for AI-built software.

ReleaseBound is a local-first macOS application that independently verifies release-readiness facts — build, tests, source state, signing, and packaging — for a software project you choose. It runs entirely on your Mac and does not trust an unverified success claim from an AI agent or from you: every required gate is checked against real output, and any outcome other than an independently confirmed pass fails closed.
