Before you contact support

  • Confirm you selected the correct project folder using “Choose Project Folder…”.
  • If a previously authorized folder was moved or renamed, choose it again to refresh access.
  • Check that required tools (such as git and Xcode's command line tools) are installed and on your Mac.

ReleaseBound support is handled by the developer directly. Any information you voluntarily send in a support email (such as your email address, message content, or attachments) is received by the developer in order to respond to your request.
